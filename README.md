# Frieren: Beyond Journey's End
![Alt text](https://tse2.mm.bing.net/th/id/OIP.y3TteMc18Vwbs0R0teIp9QHaKm?r=0&rs=1&pid=ImgDetMain&o=7&rm=3)
**Frieren: Beyond Journey's End** is a fantasy drama that begins **after the Hero Party defeats the Demon King** and restores peace to the realm. 

The story follows **Frieren, an immortal elven mage** who perceives time much differently than her mortal companions. After the passing of her former party member, the Hero Himmel, Frieren is struck by regret over how little she truly knew him. To better understand humans, she embarks on a **new, decades-long journey retracing her old steps**, learning to cherish fleeting connections along the way.
